## Product

- Mobile-first always.
- Accessibility is required.
- Calm UX over feature density.
- No unsafe medical claims.
- No diagnostic workflows.
- No EHR replacement positioning.
- No emergency-service positioning.
- Permissions-first architecture.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity.
- Reusable components only after real repetition.
- Strict typing enforced.
- Architecture changes require review.

## Engineering

- Do not duplicate AI-OS shared systems.
- Do not add broad agents with repository-wide write authority.
- Do not place durable domain rules inside route components.
- Do not add backend tables without RLS design.
- Do not add analytics that captures sensitive care details by default.
- Do not introduce a new framework or service without a decision record.
- Do not make golden-flow changes without verification notes.

## Copy Safety

Use:

- organize
- coordinate
- log
- remind
- share
- prepare

Avoid:

- diagnose
- prescribe
- treat
- monitor as a medical service
- clinically validate
- replace provider judgment
