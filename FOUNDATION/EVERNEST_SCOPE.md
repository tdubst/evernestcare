## In Scope

- Family care coordination
- Shared care tasks
- Medication and vitals logging
- Appointment planning
- Document organization
- Family/provider contact coordination
- Permission-aware sharing
- Gentle reminders
- Plain-language preparation for visits
- Auditability for sensitive access changes

## Out of Scope

- EHR replacement
- Clinical charting system
- Diagnosis
- Treatment recommendations
- Medication prescribing
- Telemedicine
- Emergency response
- Clinical decision support
- Automated medical interpretation

## Positioning

Evernest helps families coordinate care around a loved one. It stores and organizes user-entered information, but it does not determine clinical meaning or replace professional medical advice.
