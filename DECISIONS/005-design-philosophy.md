# 005: Design Philosophy

Status: Accepted

## Context

Evernest should reduce caregiver stress without becoming visually clinical or operationally vague.

## Decision

Use calm, premium, cognitively lightweight UI with clear hierarchy and operational reliability.

## Direction

- Prefer simple surfaces over dense dashboards.
- Use warm neutral foundations with restrained accent colors.
- Treat medical-adjacent warnings carefully; avoid alarmist styling unless safety requires it.
- Reuse shadcn/Radix primitives and Evernest tokens.
- Copy should support care coordination, not clinical interpretation.

## Anti-Patterns

- Hospital chart aesthetics
- Enterprise dashboard overload
- AI-first gimmicks
- Dense settings before users understand the care model
