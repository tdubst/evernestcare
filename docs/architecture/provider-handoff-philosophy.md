# Provider Handoff Philosophy

Provider handoff exists to compress family care continuity into a factual, scan-friendly format. It is not clinical decision support and does not interpret care.

## Readability Principles

A provider should quickly understand:

- what happened
- what changed
- what continuity gaps exist
- what artifacts are attached
- who contributed context

The handoff should prioritize concise sections over raw data volume.

## Continuity Compression

Provider summaries should group operational history into:

- provider scan
- operational summary
- continuity attention
- background profile
- attached care artifacts

Compression must be deterministic and event-derived. The same event history and timeframe should produce the same handoff.

## Appointment Preparation

Caregivers should be able to review a summary before the visit, confirm key artifacts, and scan recent attention signals. Export formatting should remain lightweight until real sharing infrastructure is needed.

## Boundaries

Provider handoff must not include:

- diagnosis generation
- treatment recommendations
- predictive interpretation
- hidden AI conclusions
- provider portal workflows
- clinical decision support
