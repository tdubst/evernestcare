# Export Share Model

Export and share support exists to help caregivers prepare factual continuity packets. It should remain lightweight, deterministic, and appointment-oriented.

## Philosophy

Provider-facing exports are projections of operational history, care profile context, and selected care artifacts. They should be easy to print, review, and hand to a provider without implying Evernest Care is a provider portal or medical record system.

## Supported Initial Shape

- Printable provider summaries.
- Shareable continuity snapshots.
- Appointment-ready summaries.
- Artifact references included as factual attachments.

Initial exports may be rendered in-app as structured sections. PDF export and platform share sheets are future delivery mechanisms, not separate sources of truth.

## Continuity Packet Contents

A continuity packet may include:

- operational summary for a timeframe
- recent medication events
- vitals events
- collaborative notes
- selected artifact references
- background care profile facts

All content must be deterministic and derived from current projections.

## Boundaries

Exports must not include:

- treatment recommendations
- diagnosis generation
- predictive conclusions
- hidden AI interpretation
- provider portal collaboration features
- enterprise medical records workflows

## Future Pathways

Future implementation may add PDF export, mobile share sheet support, provider snapshots, and appointment-specific continuity packets. Those pathways should continue to treat the event runtime as source authority.
