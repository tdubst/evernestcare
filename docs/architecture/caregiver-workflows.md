# Caregiver Workflows

EvernestCare optimizes for continuity confidence, not task overload. Workflows should help families understand what happened, what changed, and what is ready for the next handoff.

## Canonical Workflows

### Medication Management

Continuity touchpoints:

- medication schedule
- taken or missed events
- actor attribution
- recent confirmation gaps

Goal: make adherence history easy to confirm without creating clinical recommendations.

### Appointment Preparation

Continuity touchpoints:

- provider summary
- relevant artifacts
- recent vitals
- caregiver notes
- continuity signals

Goal: help a caregiver enter a visit with a factual, scan-friendly packet.

### Post-Discharge Continuity

Continuity touchpoints:

- discharge artifacts
- follow-up paperwork
- medication changes
- caregiver observations
- care-circle participation

Goal: keep discharge context attached to the operational timeline.

### Symptom Or Operational Concern Escalation

Continuity touchpoints:

- attributed notes
- timeframe context
- related vitals or medication events
- follow-up visibility

Goal: surface concern context for human review without diagnosis or triage automation.

### Provider Handoff

Continuity touchpoints:

- compressed operational summary
- continuity attention signals
- selected artifacts
- background care profile

Goal: answer quickly what changed, what matters, and what may need follow-up.

### Collaborative Caregiving

Continuity touchpoints:

- care-circle actors
- attributed actions
- timeline participation
- shared artifacts and notes

Goal: make shared care legible without adding enterprise workflow complexity.

## UX Principles

- Keep workflows short and inspectable.
- Show next steps as operational suggestions, not clinical instructions.
- Preserve attribution.
- Prefer calm copy over urgent alerts.
- Avoid healthcare bureaucracy and dense task systems.
