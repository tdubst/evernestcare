# Beta Readiness Principles

Beta readiness for EvernestCare means caregivers can trust the continuity record during normal family coordination. It does not require feature breadth.

## Stability Philosophy

The beta product should protect:

- deterministic replay
- projection integrity
- append-only operational history
- calm first-use experience
- clear handoff and provider summary surfaces

Every visible continuity claim should be traceable to typed operational state.

## Trust-First UX

Beta UX should feel:

- calm
- emotionally supportive
- lightweight
- organized
- factual

Caregivers should understand what changed without interpreting a dashboard.

## Replay Integrity Standards

For beta, reducers must remain deterministic, projections must not mutate event history, and UI state must not become operational truth.

Verification should cover:

- medication actions
- vitals actions
- timeline rendering
- provider summaries
- care artifacts
- continuity signals
- onboarding path

## Onboarding Confidence

Onboarding should get a caregiver to the first useful continuity record quickly:

1. care subject
2. care circle
3. medications or basic history
4. timeline context
5. provider-ready summary

Avoid giant intake workflows and premature setup requirements.
