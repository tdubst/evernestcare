# Vitals Visualization Philosophy

Vitals visualization in Evernest Care exists for continuity awareness. Charts should help a caregiver and provider quickly understand recent trends without turning the app into a clinical monitoring dashboard.

## Principles

- Separate trends by vital type.
- Keep charts mobile-readable.
- Show enough history to support handoffs.
- Avoid dense analytics and complicated interactions.
- Preserve event-derived operational truth.

## Blood Pressure

Blood pressure should show systolic and diastolic together so a caregiver can see direction and recent stability. The chart should support provider conversation, not interpretation or treatment guidance.

## Heart Rate

Heart rate should show operational trend visibility. It should help caregivers discuss context such as rest, activity, or recent events without implying clinical assessment.

## Weight

Weight should support longitudinal continuity, especially post-discharge and recovery contexts. It should be readable as a simple trend, not a diagnostic signal.

## Non-Goals

- Clinician monitoring dashboards.
- Advanced analytics tooling.
- Predictive alerts.
- Medical interpretation.
- Dense chart interactions.
