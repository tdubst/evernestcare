# Mobile Continuity UX

Evernest Care is mobile-first because caregiving often happens between appointments, errands, calls, and family handoffs.

## Principles

- Keep primary actions reachable with one hand.
- Use short cards with clear labels.
- Make timelines easy to scan.
- Keep provider summaries structured and compact.
- Use calm visual hierarchy.
- Preserve accessibility controls.

## Continuity Readability

Mobile screens should answer:

- who is being cared for
- what needs review
- what happened recently
- what is ready for a provider
- what documents are attached

Cards should reveal enough context to act without forcing long reading sessions.

## Emotional UX

The product should reduce caregiver stress. Avoid alarm-heavy copy, dense dashboards, or hospital-style visual language. Operational gaps should feel manageable and inspectable.

## Interaction Guidance

- Prefer buttons with familiar icons for quick actions.
- Keep segmented filters horizontally scrollable when space is tight.
- Keep copy short on cards.
- Avoid nested cards and dense dashboard grids.
- Preserve stable dimensions for repeated rows.

## Non-Goals

- Dashboard overload.
- Alert fatigue.
- Enterprise monitoring surfaces.
- Complex administrative workflows.
